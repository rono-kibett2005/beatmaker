Install in four steps

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded BeatMaker.apk file.
  3. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
  4. Open BeatMaker and tap a pad.

Questions

Does it need internet?

No. Every sound is built into the app or stored on your phone.

Where are my exported songs?

In your phone's Music folder, inside a folder called BeatMaker.

Why does Android warn me before installing?

The app is installed from this site instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to confirm.
